# Vire demo film v3 script

**Format:** 1920×1080, 30 fps, under 1:58. The film follows one recorded face through the real phone sensing path, the live mirror, the dose, the re-measure, and the clinic view.

## Voice-over and picture

1. **Hook:** “This phone just played a laugh. Then it timed the smile that came back.” The live mirror fills the frame; the wireframe lights up as the laughter plays. The onset time is readable.
2. **Problem and name:** “This is Vire. One minute a day at home, after TMS treatment for depression.” Show the phone and the phone's real screen. “TMS is magnetic brain stimulation: 20 to 30 clinic sessions over 4 to 6 weeks.”
3. **Gap:** “Within a year, TMS stops working for about half the people it helped. Vire shows the clinic who to call back.” Show the clinic list and the 46.3% claim.
4. **Check-in:** “The minute is four small things.” Show each as its own readable shot: Nod, Laugh, Speak, Breathe. The phone and mirror show the product's live result.
5. **Echo:** “Then the echo. Each reading is scored against your own usual, so 50 is a normal day.” Show the result with all four channels at readable size. “It's a measurement, not a diagnosis.”
6. **Dose and re-measure:** “After the echo, Vire offers one small thing. Here, a forest in your ears: turn your head to find the birds.” Show the dose. “Then it plays the laugh again and measures again. It keeps count of what followed. It can't yet tell what helped.” Show before/after values and the product's second measurement.
7. **Checked so far, then clinic climax:** Show the sensing checks before returning to the product. “The clinic gets numbers, never video or sound. Three low days in a row, or you go quiet, and your name moves up.” Show the short list, one patient's latest ping second by second, the dose chart, and “Request booster session.” “These patients are simulated. This is what slipping would look like. One tap asks them back in.”
8. **Close:** “One minute a day, so your clinic knows when to call.” End on Vire and the measured-loop caveat.

The only source label for the fixture is: “Recorded face, with stand-in AirPods readings for head and heart, played into the live product.” AirPods are shown only where the product itself labels head and heart; their accuracy is not claimed. The clinic cohort is explicitly simulated.
