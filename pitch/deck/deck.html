The problem

After TMS, the benefit fades at home. The clinic doesn’t see you there.

A course is 20 to 30 clinic visits over 4 to 6 weeks. Then you go home.

When people got worse, more TMS brought the benefit back for 32 of 38, in an open study with no control group (Janicak 2010).

Senova et al. 2019, Brain Stimulation. The one-year figure is from 9 of its 19 studies, 247 patients (95% CI 33 to 61%).

Sense

Vire4
Respond

A sea in your ears that slows only when your breath follows.

It rises as you breathe in and falls as you breathe out, stepping toward six breaths a minute. When the breath can’t be read, it waits for a still head.

Measure, then learn

It checks you before and after, and learns what helps you.

“Calmer after” needs a lower heart rate; a stiller head alone is “Stiller after”. “Vire learned” comes only at 80% sure, after 3 scored runs.

46 s
My own setup minutes

3 minutes on my AirPods. A network-blocked Modal Sandbox tried 366 models. On a whole minute it never saw: nod 0.97, head shake 0.97, smile 0.64, too weak to count yet.

50 at once
The whole clinic at once

50 simulated patients, one Sandbox each, all running together: around 10,000 models in about half a minute, and each re-learned which practice helps that patient.

Scores: 1.00 is never wrong, 0.50 is luck. A shared model pretrained on a public earbud dataset did not clearly win, so each person keeps their own.
