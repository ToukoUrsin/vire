Mics are cut at 3:00 sharp, then 3 minutes of questions. About 382 spoken words plus the moves. Planned end about 2:50; if you speak slowly, use the cuts below. Have a teammate show you the clock at "There's also a body scan": it should say about 1:45. Later than 1:55, use the cuts below.

## 0:00 AirPods in (10 s)

**Screen:** the iPhone, Today, the day card.
**Do:** put the AirPods in on stage.

> These are just my AirPods. While they're in, an app called Vire follows how my head moves and my heart rate. The camera is off.

## 0:10 Problem (25 s)

> TMS is magnetic brain stimulation for depression. Twenty to thirty clinic visits, then you go home.
> A year later it has stopped working for about half the people it helped. In one study, people who got worse had TMS again, and 32 of 38 got the benefit back. No control group. But once you're home, the clinic doesn't see you.

## 0:35 The idea (20 s)

> Vire is for those months at home. Your AirPods notice when your days change, play a breathing practice in your ears and measure you before and after. If your days keep looking different, your clinic sees it.

## 0:55 The inner loop (50 s)

**Screen:** the day card, then this morning's run on the big screen (`?practice=1`).

> This is my day while the AirPods were in: how long my head was down, how much it moved, my walks, compared with my usual. When that drifts, Vire offers a practice.
> This morning I did mine. First a minute of smiles, nods and head shakes, then twenty seconds still while the AirPods read my head movement and heart rate.
> Then breathing. A sea in my ears rises as I breathe in and falls as I breathe out. It slows toward six breaths a minute, but only when I keep up. The AirPods feel my head move with each breath, and when they can't, it says so.
> Then the same check again. Heart rate **[REAL: heart]**. Head movement **[REAL: still]**.

## 1:45 Then it learns (20 s)

> There's also a body scan and a short walk. Each practice is scored by what the AirPods measured, and Vire learns which one settles me most. Until it has enough, it says "too early to say".

## 2:05 The outer loop (25 s)

**Screen:** `#/clinic`, the top of the list.

> If my days keep looking different after the practices, I go to the top of my clinic's list, with what Vire tried and what it measured. *(open the patient)* The clinic can book a TMS booster now, not on a fixed schedule. Whether earlier is better, a pilot has to test. These patients are simulated.

## 2:30 Modal (12 s)

**Screen:** `#/clinic/live?cohort=1`. **Do:** press "Re-train the whole clinic" as you open the tab and talk over it. Don't wait for it to finish.

> It runs on Modal's cloud. Each patient's data sits in its own locked box, and Vire re-learns what helps all 50 simulated patients in half a minute.

## 2:42 Close (10 s)

> No patient has used Vire yet, and it doesn't diagnose anything. It's there so that when your days change at home, your clinic sees it. That's Vire.

## If you're running long

- Skip the Modal press; say the line over the clinic list (5 s).
- Skip "There's also a body scan and a short walk" (3 s).
- Never cut "no control group", "these patients are simulated", "no patient has used Vire yet" or "it doesn't diagnose anything".
- If this morning's run didn't happen, say "this is the run with stand-in AirPods from our tests" where the script says "This morning I did mine".

## If something breaks on stage

- **Heart rate didn't come from the AirPods** this morning: drop "and heart rate from the AirPods" and say only the head movement.
- **The result said "No change today" or "Not calmer today":** say it. "It says what it saw. That's the point."
- **The re-train run is slow:** "It's still going. Last night it was 50 patients in about half a minute." Move on.
- **AirPods drop:** you don't need them live after 0:10. Keep going.

## What not to say

- That AirPods read the brain, EEG or HRV. They read head motion and, on AirPods Pro 3, heart rate in beats a minute.
- That a practice is a treatment, or that Vire lowers depression.
- That drift comes before relapse, or that an earlier booster works better.
- That the AirPods read smiles. The camera keeps smiles for now.
- That it senses only while Vire is open. Say: your AirPods sense every step, all the time they're in. Asked about a locked phone: that's the next iPhone build.
- Any number that isn't on your screen this morning or in `claims.md`.
