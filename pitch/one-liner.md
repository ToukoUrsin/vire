# Vire in one line

Like Meru Health, but your AirPods sense every step. For people at home after TMS, a clinic treatment for depression.

If they want the rest: while your AirPods are in, Vire sets your day against your own usual. When it drifts, it plays a short practice in your ears, measures what it did and learns what works for you. When the practices don't fix it, your clinic sees you first and can book a TMS booster. A signal for the clinic, not a diagnosis.
