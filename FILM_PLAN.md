# Vire film plan

## SHOT LIST (max 2:00, aim 1:52)

**Setup:** Laptop full screen, three tabs open: 1 `vire.fly.dev/#/clinic/live?practice=1`, 2 `vire.fly.dev/#/clinic`, 3 `vire.fly.dev/#/clinic/live?cohort=1`. iPhone with the Vire app on Today, AirPods out of the case, in your hand. Second phone on a stand, filming.

**Quiet shots:** in shots 4 and 5 Vire is measuring, so don't talk. Say those lines afterwards, over the picture. Shot 9 too: the run takes half a minute. Burn in every line as a caption.

1. **0:00** Your hand putting the AirPods in, then the iPhone. Say: "These are just my AirPods. Vire runs while they're in."
2. **0:08** The day card on the iPhone. Say: "It's for people at home after TMS, a depression treatment. Within a year about half lose the benefit, and once you're home the clinic doesn't see you."
3. **0:20** Point at [REAL: day line] on the day card (pick it in the off-camera run: the head-down line if there is one). Say: "My AirPods watch my day against my own usual." Then read the line as it is on the screen.
4. **0:30** Tap Start. Nod, shake your head, smile, sit still, as the screen asks. The check is about 1.5 minutes: film all of it, keep 12 s (a smile, a nod, a shake, sitting still). Quiet. Line for after: "Every day has a practice; when I'm off my usual, Vire offers one too. No camera. A quick check, then I sit still. Heart rate comes from the AirPods."
5. **0:42** The breathing, sea in your ears (film all of it, keep 15 s). Quiet. Line for after: "I breathe with the sea. The sound is made for me from what my AirPods just read, and it only slows down when I keep up, or, when it can't feel my breath, when I sit still."
6. **0:58** The result screen. Say: "Same check after. Heart rate [REAL: heart], head movement [REAL: still]. It learns which practice works for me."
7. **1:12** Laptop, tab 1: the practice has landed on the big screen. Say: "That run was real. The clinic sees what Vire tried and what changed. The other patients are simulated."
8. **1:20** Laptop, tab 2: click the top patient (Aino K.), then click "Book a booster session". Say: "When the practices don't help, a patient goes to the top of the clinic's list, with what was tried. The clinic can book a TMS booster now, not on a schedule."
9. **1:36** Laptop, tab 3: click "Re-train the whole clinic", film the click and until the boxes fill (about 30 s), keep the click and the last 5 s. Line for after: "Each patient's health data gets its own locked box on Modal's cloud. The whole clinic re-learns in about half a minute. The sea is made there too."
10. **1:48** You, AirPods in, URL on screen. Say: "That's Vire. It's live at vire.fly.dev"

Caption "Simulated patients" on shots 8 and 9. After shot 8, click "Cancel booking" off camera.

## Before you film

1. Teaching minute done once (`/#/ears`). The band of your usual in the check-in shows from your third minute.
2. Do one full Today run off camera. Note the slots from its result screen.
3. Check the day card says "Your usual comes from N earlier days" and shows a head-down line. It needs an earlier day with 30+ minutes worn with Vire open. If it says "Only today so far", read that line instead: "Only today so far: my usual starts tomorrow." Scroll so the card fills the screen for shot 3, and back up for Start in shot 4.
4. Record the laptop screen at the same time (QuickTime).
5. The breathing is two and a half minutes. Film all of it, keep 15 seconds. Don't touch the phone between Start and the result: if you do, the run starts over (about 5 minutes).
6. Laptop: open Aino K. on tab 2. If it says "Booster booked", click "Cancel booking". On tab 3 press "Re-train the whole clinic" once off camera and check it ends with all 50 boxes (tab 3 opens on the last result).

## If something doesn't work on the day

- **The day card offers no practice:** tap Start anyway. The day's plan always has one. Keep the line in shot 4.
- **Heart rate not from the AirPods** (the screen names another source or none): drop "Heart rate comes from the AirPods" from shot 4 and say only what the result screen says, for example "my head sat N% stiller".
- **The result says "No change today" or "Not calmer today":** keep it. Say "It says what it saw."
- **The sound didn't step down:** say "It stays at my pace when my breath doesn't follow."
- **The result says the sound "went by how still you sat"** (the breath could not be read): keep the line in shot 5, it says both.
- **The practice doesn't land on the big screen:** reload `#/clinic/live?practice=1` and send it again, or skip shot 7.
- **AirPods drop:** back in the case for two seconds, out again.
- **Re-training says a box could not finish:** re-shoot, press again. Say the number on the screen.
