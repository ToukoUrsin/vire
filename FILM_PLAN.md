## Before you film

1. Teaching minute done once (`/#/ears`). The band of your usual in the check-in shows from your third minute.
2. Do one full Today run off camera. Note the slots from its result screen.
3. Check the day card says "Your usual comes from N earlier days" and shows a head-down line. It needs an earlier day with 30+ minutes worn with Vire open. If it says "Only today so far", read that line instead: "Only today so far: my usual starts tomorrow." Scroll so the card fills the screen for shot 3, and back up for Start in shot 4.
4. Record the laptop screen at the same time (QuickTime).
5. The breathing is two and a half minutes. Film all of it, keep 15 seconds. Don't touch the phone between Start and the result: if you do, the run starts over (about 5 minutes).
6. Laptop: open Aino K. on tab 2. If it says "Booster booked", click "Cancel booking". On tab 3 press "Re-train the whole clinic" once off camera and check it ends with all 50 boxes (tab 3 opens on the last result).

## If something doesn't work on the day

- **The day card offers no practice:** tap Start anyway. The day's plan always has one. Keep the line in shot 4.
- **Heart rate not from the AirPods** (the screen names another source or none): drop "Heart rate comes from the AirPods" from shot 4 and say only what the result screen says, for example "my head sat N% stiller".
- **The result says "No change today" or "Not calmer today":** keep it. Say "It says what it saw."
- **The sound didn't step down:** say "It stays at my pace when my breath doesn't follow."
- **The result says the sound "went by how still you sat"** (the breath could not be read): keep the line in shot 5, it says both.
- **Your own run didn't happen before filming:** in shot 7 say "This is the scripted run" instead of "That run was real", and drop the numbers from shot 6.
- **The practice doesn't land on the big screen:** reload `#/clinic/live?practice=1` and send it again, or skip shot 7.
- **The opening's sound doesn't please:** use `open_clean.mp4` plus your voice, or mute `open.mp4` (the type stays).
- **AirPods drop:** back in the case for two seconds, out again.
- **Re-training says a box could not finish:** re-shoot, press again. Say the number on the screen.
